UK Student Visa from Pakistan in 2026: Complete Guide

Last updated: 25 June 2026

By Haiwad Ahmad · 30 April 2026 · 11 min read

Around 14,000 Pakistani students were granted UK Student Visas in 2024, making Pakistan the UK's 6th-largest student visa origin globally. The UK Student Visa process is well-documented but has tightened in 2024–2025 (with the 2024 Student Route reform stopping most students from bringing dependants). This guide is current to 2026 rules.

Step 1 — Receive an Offer from a Licensed UK Sponsor

You must first apply to and receive an unconditional offer from a UK university or college that holds a Student Sponsor Licence (verified at gov.uk's register of licensed sponsors). All major UK universities are licensed; private colleges should be checked.

Step 2 — CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies)

Once your offer is unconditional and you've paid the required deposit (usually 50% of first-year tuition), your sponsor issues a CAS letter — a unique 14-digit reference number issued via the UK Home Office system. You cannot apply for the visa without this number.

Step 3 — Meet the financial requirement

You must show you have:

  • Full first-year tuition (varies by course — typically GBP 15,000 – 35,000 for international students)
  • Living costs — GBP 1,483/month for London (12 months max) or GBP 1,136/month for outside London. So GBP 17,796 for London / GBP 13,632 for outside London.
  • Funds held for 28 consecutive days before application
  • Bank statement no older than 31 days at submission

Acceptable evidence: Pakistani bank statement, student loan letter from a regulated lender, parent's bank statement (with affidavit of support), or a sponsor's letter from a UK or recognised international scholarship body.

Step 4 — English language requirement

  • IELTS Academic for Higher Education — typically 6.0 overall minimum, no band below 5.5 (varies by course; some require 6.5–7.5)
  • Pakistani A-Levels grade C+ in English Language or HSSC equivalent may be accepted by some sponsors
  • Approved alternative tests: Pearson PTE, TOEFL iBT, Trinity GESE/ISE, LanguageCert
  • Tests must be taken at a UKVI-approved Secure English Language Test (SELT) centre

Step 5 — Apply online + book biometrics

  1. Complete the online application at gov.uk/apply-uk-visa
  2. Pay the visa fee: GBP 524 (~PKR 196,000 at 2026 rates)
  3. Pay the Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS): GBP 776/year (~PKR 290,000/year) — for a 3-year UG degree, that's PKR 870,000+ upfront
  4. Book a biometrics appointment at VFS Karachi (Clifton) or VFS Islamabad (Blue Area)
  5. Attend in person with original documents

Step 6 — Documents to bring to VFS

  • Passport (valid for whole duration of visa + 1 month)
  • Original CAS letter (printed PDF acceptable)
  • Original financial evidence (bank statements with stamp)
  • Original English test certificate
  • Original academic transcripts (some sponsors require these even after CAS)
  • Polio vaccination certificate (mandatory for Pakistanis to enter the UK in 2024–2025)
  • TB test certificate (mandatory for Pakistanis at IOM-approved Pakistani centres)
  • Parent / sponsor affidavit (if relying on parent funds)
  • Marriage certificate (if applying as dependant — but note 2024 reform limits dependants severely)

2024 reform — dependants restriction

Since January 2024, UK Student Visa holders can NO LONGER bring dependants (spouse, children) on their Student Visa UNLESS they are studying a PhD or postgraduate research course. This is a significant change from pre-2024. Master's students with families are particularly affected.

Common rejection reasons

  1. Financial evidence not held for 28 consecutive days — the most common rejection. Even one day of dipping below the threshold restarts the 28-day clock.
  2. Bank statement older than 31 days at submission
  3. Discrepancy between sponsor's records and visa application (different course name, different intake date)
  4. Credibility interview failures — UKVI sometimes calls applicants for a phone or video interview. Inability to explain why you chose your course, university, or city is a frequent rejection ground.
  5. Forged or altered documents — instant 10-year ban
  6. Prior UK visa refusal not declared
  7. Missing TB test certificate (Pakistan is on the TB-screening list)

TB test in Pakistan — IOM centres

Pakistani applicants must complete a TB test at an IOM (International Organization for Migration) approved centre before the UK visa application. Centres in Pakistan:

  • IOM Karachi — Defence area
  • IOM Islamabad — F-7
  • IOM Lahore — Gulberg

Fee: ~USD 75 (PKR 21,000). Certificate valid 6 months.

Working rights during study

  • 20 hours/week during term-time if enrolled in a degree-level programme at a Higher Education Provider with a track record of compliance
  • Full-time during vacation
  • No work at all if you're studying below-degree level
  • No self-employment, no agency work for sports professionals, no permanent full-time post

Post-study Graduate Route

After completing your UK degree, you can apply for the Graduate Route (Post-Study Work Visa) which lets you stay and work for 2 years (UG/Master's) or 3 years (PhD) without a job sponsor. This is the standard pathway from study to skilled work in the UK.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much money do I need to show for a UK Student Visa from Pakistan?

+

First-year tuition (typically GBP 15,000–35,000) + 12 months of living costs (GBP 13,632 outside London / GBP 17,796 in London). Funds must be held for 28 consecutive days, in a bank statement no older than 31 days at submission. For a typical Master's in London, expect to show GBP 50,000+ in evidence.

What IELTS score is needed for UK Student Visa?

+

For degree-level study, generally 6.0 overall with no band below 5.5. Specific universities and courses may require higher (6.5–7.5 for medicine, law, journalism). Pre-sessional and foundation courses can sometimes accept lower scores.

How long does a UK Student Visa take to process from Pakistan in 2026?

+

Standard processing is approximately 3 weeks. Priority service (GBP 500 extra) is 5 working days. Super Priority (GBP 1,000 extra) is 1 working day.

Can I bring my family on a UK Student Visa from Pakistan?

+

Only if you're pursuing a PhD or government-sponsored programme of 9+ months. Since January 2024, taught Master's students and undergraduate students can no longer bring dependants.

Can I work during my UK Student Visa?

+

Yes — 20 hours per week during term time, full-time during vacation, if enrolled in a degree-level course at a tracked Higher Education Provider. Below-degree-level study has no work rights.

What happens after I finish my UK degree?

+

You can apply for the Graduate Route (Post-Study Work Visa) within 2 months of finishing — 2 years for UG/Master's, 3 years for PhD. After that, you'd need a Skilled Worker Visa sponsored by a UK employer to continue.
