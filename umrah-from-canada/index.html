Umrah from Canada: 2026 Guide for Pakistani-Canadian Pilgrims

Last updated: 13 July 2026

The Pakistani-Canadian community is one of the fastest-growing diaspora populations in North America — ~250,000 people concentrated in the Greater Toronto Area (Mississauga, Brampton, Markham), Greater Vancouver (Surrey, Burnaby), Calgary, Edmonton, and Ottawa. Al Bari Travel serves Pakistani-Canadian families through our remote-first model with full WhatsApp + email support across Canadian time zones.

What's unique about Umrah from Canada

  • Toronto / Mississauga / Brampton (the 'Peel Region') has the highest concentration of Pakistani-Canadian families — most of our Canadian bookings originate from this area.
  • Pakistani-Canadians often book through community mosques (ISNA, MAC, Khalid bin Walid in Toronto) — we welcome mosque group inquiries with dedicated tour leaders.
  • Many Pakistani-Canadians have aging parents still in Pakistan — multi-generational Hajj + Umrah trips combining the Canadian family with Pakistan-side grandparents are common; we coordinate both legs.
  • Saudi Tourist e-Visa for Canadian passport holders is instant via visa.visitsaudi.com (around CAD 195 including insurance).
  • Canadian-side travel insurance is recommended (Manulife, RBC, Sun Life all offer Umrah-friendly policies).

Frequently asked questions

Do you have a Canadian representative?

+

We don't have a Canada-based Regional Representative yet, but we serve Pakistani-Canadian families remotely with full WhatsApp + email coverage during Canadian business hours. Our Texas Regional Rep (Hamid Ali) handles North American time zones.

Can you book for Canadian citizens who aren't Pakistani?

+

Yes. We serve any Canadian resident, regardless of passport or background. We book Umrah for Canadian Muslims from many backgrounds.

How are payments handled from Canada?

+

Wire transfer, Interac e-Transfer (Canadian account), or via Wise to our Pakistan bank account. CAD-denominated invoice.

Can my elderly Pakistani parents join us via Pakistan?

+

Yes — we coordinate combined bookings where Canadian family flies from Toronto/Vancouver, Pakistani parents fly from Lahore/Karachi/Islamabad, both meeting in Madinah or Makkah on aligned dates. Common scenario for us.

Do I need any Canadian-side vaccinations?

+

Meningococcal ACWY is required for Saudi (any nationality). Polio booster is required only for travellers who've been to polio-endemic countries (Pakistan, Afghanistan) within 12 months of Saudi entry. Most Canadian residents don't need it.
