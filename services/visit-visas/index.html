Overview

What this service covers

Whether you are planning a family visit to relatives abroad or a leisure trip, Al Bari Travel handles the visa application from document preparation through embassy submission and tracking. We support tourist and family-visit applications for all major destinations: Saudi Arabia (e-Visa tourist + Umrah-eligible), UAE, Schengen Area (29 European countries on one visa), UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Malaysia, Singapore, Turkey, and Thailand.

What's included

  • Application form completion (online for e-Visa countries, paper + online for traditional embassies)
  • Document checklist customized to your destination and travel purpose
  • Invitation letter verification and supporting documents (for family-visit visas — host status proof, address, relationship documents)
  • Bank statement preparation guidance (typical requirement: 6 months of statements, 'show money' balance varies by destination — usually USD 5,000–10,000 equivalent for Schengen/UK/Canada)
  • Employment / educational documentation
  • Document attestation where required (HEC, MoFA, destination embassy)
  • Travel insurance coordination — mandatory for Schengen (minimum €30,000 coverage), recommended for UK and other destinations
  • Hotel booking and itinerary preparation (most visit visas require proof of accommodation + onward travel)
  • Embassy / VFS Global appointment booking
  • Status tracking through to embassy decision
  • Re-application support if first attempt is unsuccessful

Common Questions

Visit Visas — FAQ

Which countries' visit visas do you process?

+

Saudi Arabia (e-Visa tourist + Umrah-eligible), UAE (30-day and 60-day tourist), Schengen (29 European countries — Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Portugal, Greece, Austria, Belgium, and 20 others), UK (Standard Visitor / Family), USA (B1/B2), Canada (TRV), Australia (subclass 600 visitor), Malaysia, Singapore, Turkey (e-Visa), Thailand.

How long does processing take?

+

Saudi e-Visa: 24–48 hours. UAE tourist: 3–5 working days. Schengen: 15–30 days standard. UK Standard Visitor: 3 weeks standard, 5 working days priority, 24 hours super-priority (additional fees apply). USA B1/B2: interview wait time is the bottleneck — currently 6–12+ months in Pakistan for new applicants at both Karachi and Islamabad consulates. Canada TRV: 2–3 weeks. Australia: 2–3 months. Apply as early as your travel dates allow.

Do I need an invitation letter for family visits?

+

Most destinations require one when visiting family — UK, Schengen, USA, Canada all do. Your host abroad sends a signed invitation letter along with proof of their immigration status (visa / residence permit / citizenship document), proof of their address (utility bill / tenancy agreement), and proof of relationship (birth certificate, marriage certificate, etc). We tell you exactly what is needed for each destination.

How much money should I show in my bank account?

+

Varies by destination. Common guidance: USD 5,000–10,000 minimum for Schengen / UK / Canada to demonstrate funds for your trip. USA looks at your overall financial profile and ties to Pakistan (job, family, property), not just bank balance. Saudi tourist e-Visa requires proof of accommodation + return ticket but has a lower bank requirement. Your Regional Representative will tell you the figure for your case.

Do I need travel insurance?

+

Mandatory for Schengen (minimum €30,000 medical coverage, valid across all Schengen countries for the visa duration). Recommended for UK, Canada, USA. Most other destinations do not require it but it is sensible. We coordinate insurance with reliable providers — typical premiums are PKR 1,500–4,000 for a 30-day trip depending on age and destination.

Can I get a multi-entry visit visa?

+

Many destinations offer multi-entry options: Saudi e-Visa is 1-year multi-entry by default. UAE has 60-day multi-entry and 5-year multi-entry options. Schengen offers multi-entry visas (up to 5 years for frequent travellers with a clean history). USA B1/B2 is typically 5-year multi-entry. UK Standard Visitor is up to 10-year multi-entry. We will recommend the right option based on your travel pattern.

What if I have been refused a visa before?

+

Past refusals must be disclosed on every future application — concealing them is grounds for permanent ban. We work with you to understand why the refusal happened (most are document-related, some are intent-related) and prepare a stronger re-application with the issues addressed. Refusal does not mean permanent block; many re-applications succeed once the underlying problem is fixed.
