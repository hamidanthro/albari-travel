Overview

What this service covers

Al Bari Travel issues international airline tickets from every major Pakistani airport — Islamabad (ISB), Lahore (LHE), Karachi (KHI), Peshawar (PEW), Multan (MUX), Faisalabad (LYP), and Sialkot (SKT). We work with all major regional carriers connected to global GDS booking systems (Amadeus, Sabre, Galileo), so you get the same fares the major OTAs do — plus a human Regional Representative who can advise on routings, stopovers, and cancellation rules. E-tickets are typically delivered within 30 minutes to 4 hours of payment during business hours.

What's included

  • One-way, return, and multi-city itineraries
  • Economy, Premium Economy, Business, and First class options (carrier-dependent)
  • All major Pakistani departure airports — including connecting flights from smaller cities via Karachi/Lahore/Islamabad hubs
  • Best-fare comparison across PIA, Saudia, Air Sial, AirBlue, Emirates, Qatar Airways, Etihad, Turkish Airlines, Oman Air, Salam Air, and others as available on your route
  • Stopover routings (e.g. Dubai, Doha, Istanbul) at no extra fare in many cases — useful for sightseeing breaks
  • E-ticket delivery within hours of payment confirmation
  • Group fare quotes for 10+ travellers (often 10–20% below published fares)
  • Re-booking and date-change assistance subject to airline fare rules
  • Cancellation assistance — we file the refund request and chase the airline on your behalf

Common Questions

Airline Tickets — FAQ

Which airlines do you book?

+

All major carriers serving Pakistan: PIA, Saudia (Saudi Airlines), Air Sial, AirBlue, Qatar Airways, Emirates, Etihad, Turkish Airlines, Oman Air, Salam Air, and others as available on your route. We use GDS systems (Amadeus, Sabre, Galileo) so we see real-time inventory and fares across all of them.

Which Pakistani airports can I fly from?

+

All major international airports: Allama Iqbal Lahore (LHE), Jinnah Karachi (KHI), Islamabad (ISB), Bacha Khan Peshawar (PEW), Multan (MUX), Faisalabad (LYP), and Sialkot (SKT). For passengers in cities without direct international service (Sukkur, Quetta, Skardu), we arrange domestic feeder flights to the nearest international hub.

How fast do I get my ticket?

+

E-tickets are typically delivered within 30 minutes to 4 hours of payment confirmation during business hours (Mon–Sat, 9 AM–8 PM PKT). Complex multi-city itineraries may take up to 24 hours. Once issued, the e-ticket arrives via WhatsApp or email and you can check the booking directly on the airline's website using the PNR.

Can I change or cancel a ticket?

+

Yes, subject to the airline's fare rules. Each fare class has its own rules (Restricted Economy = often non-refundable, Flex Economy = changes allowed with fee, Business/First = usually fully flexible). We brief you on the fare rules before you book, and we handle the change/cancellation request on your behalf if needed.

Do you offer student-fare or Hajj/Umrah group-fare discounts?

+

Yes — many airlines offer special fares: student fares (typically 10–15% off, requires student status verification), Hajj/Umrah group fares (substantially discounted, requires Hajj/Umrah package booking), labour/worker fares (some Gulf routes). Your Regional Representative will check available discounts for your dates and destination.

Can you book complex itineraries with stopovers?

+

Yes. Multi-city routings, dual-airline tickets, stopovers in Doha / Dubai / Istanbul / Bangkok for sightseeing — all standard. Some airlines (Qatar, Turkish, Emirates) offer free stopover programmes with hotel discounts at their hub cities. Worth mentioning your travel plans to the Regional Representative so we can suggest the best routing.

Why book through you instead of an online travel agency?

+

Three reasons: (1) Same fares because we use the same GDS booking systems, often slightly better due to consolidator agreements. (2) Real human help with re-booking, cancellations, and complex itineraries — OTAs put you in chat queues. (3) Local payment methods (cash, JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer) instead of being forced to use a credit card.
