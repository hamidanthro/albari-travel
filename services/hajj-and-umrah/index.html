Overview

What this service covers

Al Bari Travel has coordinated Hajj and Umrah for Pakistani and Pakistani-American families since 2018. Every Umrah package includes round-trip international flights, Saudi visa processing via the official Nusuk platform, hotel accommodation chosen for proximity to Masjid al-Haram (Makkah) and Masjid an-Nabawi (Madinah), ground transport between cities, and a guided Ziyarat tour. Hajj packages add the multi-stage rites coordination across Makkah, Mina, Arafat, Muzdalifah, and Madinah, with a licensed mu'allim (Saudi guide) attached to each group. Your Regional Representative handles every step — no call centres, no hand-offs.

Common Questions

Hajj & Umrah — FAQ

What is the difference between Hajj and Umrah?

+

Hajj is the obligatory pilgrimage every able Muslim must perform once in a lifetime, on specific days of Dhu'l-Hijjah (Hajj 2027 falls 14–19 May 2027). Umrah is non-obligatory and can be performed any time of year. From Pakistan, Umrah trips typically last 10–14 days; Hajj trips run 25–40 days because of the multi-stage rites at Makkah, Mina, Arafat, Muzdalifah, and Madinah.

What documents do I need?

+

Valid passport (6+ months from return date), recent passport-size photographs, NIC copy, Meningococcal ACYW135 vaccination certificate (mandatory for Hajj, recommended for Umrah), Polio vaccination certificate within last 6 weeks (mandatory for Pakistanis travelling to Saudi). For minors: birth certificate plus father's consent letter if mother travels alone with children. For couples: marriage certificate may be required for some package categories.

How early should I book Hajj 2027?

+

As early as possible. Pakistan's Hajj visa quota from Saudi (~179,000 seats annually, split between the Government Hajj Scheme and licensed Private Hajj Operators) fills months in advance. Premium hotels near Masjid al-Haram book out 6+ months ahead. For Hajj 2027 (mid-May 2027), confirm your booking by November–December 2026 at the latest.

How early should I book Umrah?

+

For standard Umrah, 2–4 weeks before travel is comfortable. Ramadan Umrah (busiest period) needs 6–8 weeks ahead because flights, hotels near the Haram, and visa slots are all in peak demand. Off-peak months (mid-September to mid-October, late February to early April) are easiest to book on short notice.

Are payment plans available?

+

Yes — for Hajj packages we accept staged payments (typically: confirmation deposit on booking, balance due before visa submission). For Umrah, full payment is usually required before ticket issuance because international airfares and hotel pre-pays do not allow staged structures. Your Regional Representative will walk you through specific options on the first call.

Can my elderly parents travel?

+

Yes. We arrange airport wheelchair assistance in Jeddah and Madinah, hotels closer to the Haram for those with mobility issues, slower-paced Ziyarat tours, and ground transport with assistance. Tell us your family's needs and we'll tailor the package — this is one of our most common arrangements.

Are family Umrah packages available?

+

Yes. We tailor hotel categories, room types (connecting rooms, large suites for 4–6 people), and Ziyarat pacing for families of any size — couples, parents with adult children, multi-generational groups. Tell your Regional Representative the group composition and we'll quote accordingly.

What is the Nusuk platform?

+

Nusuk is the Saudi government's official platform for Hajj and Umrah pilgrim management — visa issuance, Masjid al-Haram and Riyadh-ul-Jannah entry permits, and trip planning. We handle all Nusuk-related steps on your behalf; you do not need to register or operate the platform yourself, though you can install the Nusuk app to track your visa and entry permits.

What hotels do you book?

+

It depends on package tier. Economy: 3-star or 4-star hotels in the Misfalah area (800m–2km from Haram) — common chains include Al Olayan, Dar Al Eiman, Anjum. Premium: 4–5 star hotels in Ajyad / Abraj Al Bait towers area (50–300m from Haram) — including Hilton Suites, Anjum, Conrad, Pullman Zamzam. Hotel selection is confirmed at booking; substitutions only if a specific hotel is fully sold out.
