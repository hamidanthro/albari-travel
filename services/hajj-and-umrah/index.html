Common Questions

Hajj & Umrah — FAQ

What is the difference between Hajj and Umrah?

+

Hajj is the obligatory pilgrimage every able Muslim must perform once in their lifetime, on specific days of Dhu'l-Hijjah. Umrah is a non-obligatory pilgrimage that can be performed any time of year. From Pakistan, Umrah trips typically last 10–14 days; Hajj trips run 25–40 days because of the multi-stage rites.

What documents do I need?

+

A valid passport with at least 6 months of validity, recent passport-size photos, an NIC copy, and proof of travel (we provide this from our booking). For Hajj you also need to be vaccinated for meningitis (we coordinate this with you). Children require their own passports and NICs.

How early should I book?

+

For Umrah, 2–4 weeks before your travel date is comfortable. For Hajj, book as early as possible — 6+ months ideally — because the Saudi government allocates limited Hajj visa quotas per country and good hotel inventory near the Haram books out fast.

Are family packages available?

+

Yes. We tailor hotel categories, room types, and Ziyarat pacing for families of any size — couples, parents with adult children, and multi-generational groups. Tell your Regional Representative your group composition and we'll quote accordingly.
