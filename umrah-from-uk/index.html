Last updated: 25 June 2026

The UK has the largest Pakistani diaspora in Europe — over 1.6 million people, concentrated in Bradford, Birmingham, London, Manchester, and Luton. What's unique about Umrah from United Kingdom

  • UK Pakistani families often travel as multi-generational groups (grandparents + parents + children) — quote multi-room hotel suites accordingly.
  • Many UK pilgrims prefer to depart from Birmingham or Manchester (not just London) — Al Bari coordinates regional departures.
  • British Pakistani returning pilgrims often combine Umrah with a 7–10 day Pakistan visit — we book onward Saudi ↔ Pakistan ↔ UK multi-city tickets.
  • Polio vaccination booster is mandatory for UK travellers visiting Pakistan (NHS clinics provide; carry yellow card to Saudi too).
  • Saudi Tourist e-Visa is straightforward for UK passport holders (instant approval, multi-entry, 1 year validity).

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Pakistani passport to book through Al Bari from the UK?

+

No. We serve any UK resident, regardless of passport. We routinely book Umrah for British nationals, dual UK-Pakistani citizens, and Pakistani citizens on UK visas.

Are flight prices in GBP fixed?

+

Quoted in GBP at time of booking. Final ticket issuance uses the airline's GBP rate. Hotel and Saudi-side costs are quoted in GBP using the day's SAR/GBP rate.

Can you arrange airport pickup from a UK airport?

+

We don't handle UK-side ground transport, but we coordinate Saudi-side airport pickup at Jeddah / Madinah as standard for all package bookings.

How do payments work from the UK?

+

UK bank transfer to our Pakistan bank account (sterling-denominated invoice). Wise / Remitly transfers also accepted. No credit card processing fee — final price is the price quoted.

Is Saudi Tourist e-Visa or Umrah e-Visa better for UK residents?

+

Saudi Tourist e-Visa is generally faster and more flexible for UK passport holders (instant approval, 1-year multi-entry, 90-day stay). Umrah-specific e-Visa via Nusuk Masar is also an option (single entry, 30-day stay). We advise on the best route based on your travel plans.
