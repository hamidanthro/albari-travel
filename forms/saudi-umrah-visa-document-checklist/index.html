Saudi Umrah Visa Document Checklist for Pakistani Applicants (2026)

Last updated: 25 June 2026

By Al Bari Travel & Tours · 24 June 2026 · 3 min read

The Saudi Umrah visa application is straightforward when you have every document ready. It is a nightmare when even one item is missing or incorrect. This checklist covers every document required for Pakistani applicants in 2026, across both the traditional Pakistani agent route and the direct Nusuk Masar e-Visa route.

The 8 core documents required

1. Original Machine-Readable Passport (MRP)

Validity at least 6 months beyond your scheduled exit from Saudi Arabia. If yours is borderline, renew at any DGI&P Passport Office BEFORE applying. MRP and e-Passport both accepted; non-MRP old passports are NOT accepted.

2. Passport-size photograph (2 copies)

  • 4 cm wide x 6 cm tall
  • White background, plain
  • Recent (within 6 months)
  • Face fully visible, no smiling, no head turned
  • Men: no head covering. Women: hijab acceptable, face must be visible
  • No glasses (especially tinted)

3. Polio vaccination yellow card

Mandatory for all Pakistani travellers. Must be administered 28 days to 12 months before Saudi arrival. Free at any government EPI centre. Full polio guide here.

4. Meningococcal ACWY vaccination certificate

Required for all Umrah and Hajj pilgrims globally. Valid for 3 years. NOT free — PKR 4,000-6,500 at private hospitals (Aga Khan, Doctors Hospital, Hameed Latif, etc).

5. Confirmed hotel booking in Makkah

Required: hotel name, address, dates of stay, booking confirmation number. Many Pakistani Umrah packages include this. If self-booking via Nusuk Masar, use Booking.com or Almosafer for confirmation.

6. Confirmed hotel booking in Madinah

Same as Makkah — need name, address, dates, confirmation number. If staying in Makkah only, you can skip this; if visiting Madinah at any point during Umrah trip, hotel proof is needed.

7. Confirmed return air ticket

Round-trip air ticket booking confirmation. Saudi authorities want assurance you will leave the country by the visa expiry. Single-leg tickets are sometimes rejected.

8. CNIC (front and back photocopies)

Required. NICOP for overseas Pakistanis. Original CNIC also presented at the Pakistani agent office or VFS centre.

Additional documents (depending on circumstances)

  • For women under 45: Mahram affidavit + NADRA Computerised Marriage Certificate / FRC / B-Form proving relationship. Mahram affidavit guide.
  • For minors (under 18): NADRA computerised birth certificate, both parents' CNIC, and (if travelling without both parents) notarised No-Objection Certificate (NOC) from the absent parent.
  • For Nusuk Masar applications: Bank statement from last 3 months (showing financial capacity).
  • For first-time travellers: Some agents request additional verification — employer NOC or university enrollment letter.

Common reasons applications are rejected

  1. Passport validity less than 6 months (most common rejection)
  2. Photograph not meeting MoFA specifications (wrong background, glasses, smiling)
  3. Previous Saudi visa overstay (results in 3-year ban + SAR 25,000 fine)
  4. Polio vaccination outside 28-day to 12-month window
  5. Conflicting dates between hotel and ticket (e.g. ticket returns 3rd, hotel booked until 5th)
  6. Applying during Hajj-season blackout (mid-Shawwal to mid-Dhu al-Hijjah)
  7. Mahram documentation incomplete (affidavit without supporting NADRA document)
  8. Forged or altered documents — permanent blacklisting

Hajj-season blackout (CRITICAL TIMING)

Saudi MoFA suspends Umrah visa issuance from approximately Shawwal 15 to Dhu al-Hijjah 15 each Hijri year (roughly mid-May to mid-July in 2026-2027). During this window, only confirmed Hajj pilgrims can enter Saudi. Pakistani applicants planning Umrah for May or June should apply early or postpone.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can I get a Saudi Umrah visa from Pakistan?

+

Fastest is Nusuk Masar e-Visa: 24-72 hours processing if all documents are in order. Pakistani agent route via MoFA: 5-14 days. Saudi Tourist e-Visa via Pakistani embassy: 1-5 days.

Can I apply for a Saudi Umrah visa without using a Pakistani agent?

+

Yes. Direct application via Nusuk Masar (nusuk.sa) is fully available for Pakistani nationals since 2023. You upload documents online, pay SAR 535 inclusive fee, and receive the e-Visa via email in 24-72 hours.

What happens if my Saudi visa expires while I am in Saudi?

+

3-year ban from any Saudi visa, SAR 25,000 fine, and potential deportation. Always exit before stamped expiry. For emergencies (illness, family death), contact the Pakistani Embassy in Riyadh or Consulate in Jeddah immediately.

Do children need separate Saudi visas?

+

Yes. Every traveller, including infants, needs an individual Saudi visa. Children CAN be added to a parent&apos;s application file (one combined application), but each child gets a separate visa with their own visa number.

Can I get an Umrah visa for someone who has been to Israel?

+

Saudi authorities scrutinise applications from passports showing Israeli entry/exit stamps. Approval is at MoFA discretion. Always disclose travel history honestly &mdash; hiding it can result in permanent blacklisting if discovered.

My passport photograph was rejected — what do I do?

+

Get a new photograph at any professional photo studio (specify "Saudi visa photo" &mdash; they know the spec). White background, plain wall behind, no glasses, no head covering for men, hijab OK for women with face fully visible, no smiling, no head turned. Cost in Pakistan: PKR 200-500 for 6-8 prints.
