Gulf Work Visa Document Checklist for Pakistani Workers (2026)

Last updated: 25 June 2026

By Al Bari Travel & Tours · 24 June 2026 · 3 min read

Over 2 million Pakistanis work in the GCC, making Pakistan the largest source country of expatriate labour in the Gulf. The work-visa process is broadly similar across all 6 GCC countries with country-specific variations. This master checklist covers everything needed for Pakistani workers heading to Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, or Kuwait.

Stage 1: Job offer + employer-side preparation

Before YOUR paperwork starts, the Gulf-side employer must:

  1. Issue a Demand Letter / Offer Letter / Wakala (Saudi term) specifying job, salary, contract duration, accommodation arrangements
  2. Obtain approved visa quota / Block Visa / Labour Approval from their country's Labour Ministry
  3. Submit your details to relevant Gulf authority

Stage 2: Pakistani document attestation

Three-stage attestation chain for each required document:

  • Educational degree (HEC + MoFA + Embassy): For skilled positions — engineers, doctors, IT, finance, hospitality managers, teachers. See full attestation guide.
  • Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) + MoFA + Embassy: Required for most worker visas. PCC issued by District Police Office, usually 15-30 days.
  • Marriage certificate (NADRA + MoFA + Embassy): Required only if bringing family on residence visa. Use the NADRA Computerised Marriage Certificate, NOT the original Nikah-nama.
  • Children's birth certificates (NADRA + MoFA + Embassy): Required only if bringing children on family residence visa.
  • Experience certificates from previous employers: For skilled positions. Original employer attestation + MoFA + Embassy.
  • Profession-specific verifications: Pakistan Medical Commission for doctors, Pakistan Engineering Council for engineers, etc.

Stage 3: GAMCA medical examination

Mandatory for all Gulf work visas. Full GAMCA guide here.

  • Find authorised centre at wafid.com
  • Tests: HIV, Hep B/C, syphilis, TB X-ray, drug screen, pregnancy (women), ECG (over 40)
  • Cost: PKR 6,500-9,500
  • Report turnaround: 2-4 working days
  • Validity: 90 days (60 days for some Gulf countries)

Stage 4: Visa stamping at destination country embassy / consulate

With attested documents + GAMCA fitness + demand letter, apply at:

  • Saudi Embassy Islamabad / Consulate Karachi — for Saudi visas
  • UAE Embassy Islamabad / Consulate Karachi — for UAE visas
  • Qatar Embassy Islamabad / Consulate Karachi — for Qatar visas
  • etc.

Processing: 5-15 working days typical.

Stage 5: POE Protector stamp (MANDATORY)

Before flying, every Pakistani going to the Gulf for work MUST get the Protector stamp at a Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment (BEMB) office. POE Protector guide.

  • 8 POE offices: Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Multan, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Quetta, Faisalabad
  • Fee: PKR 1,000
  • Without this stamp, Pakistani airport immigration will block you from boarding the Gulf-bound flight

Country-specific variations

Saudi Arabia

  • ~2.6M Pakistani workers (largest single Gulf destination)
  • Kafala (sponsor) system under reform
  • Iqama (residence permit) issued after arrival
  • SAR 200-800 monthly Iqama fee, sponsor-paid

UAE

  • ~1.7M Pakistani workers
  • 2-month Employment Visa for entry, converted to Residence Visa
  • Tawjeeh orientation (1 day) mandatory for new arrivals
  • Emirates ID issued for residents

Qatar

  • ~200,000 Pakistani workers
  • Hayya app card required since 2022
  • Kafala reformed 2020 — workers can change jobs without sponsor permission

Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman

  • Smaller Pakistani populations
  • Country-specific permit categories
  • Each has its own labour authority

Critical worker rights to verify before signing

  • Read contract in full BEFORE leaving Pakistan
  • Wage in writing — some employers reduce wages after arrival (illegal but happens)
  • Food and accommodation arrangements
  • Keep contract copy in Pakistan with family
  • Working hours, weekly off, annual leave, return ticket entitlement
  • End-of-service gratuity terms (legally mandated in most Gulf countries)
  • Probation period (usually 90 days)
  • Refuse Arabic-only contracts without notarised translation

Avoiding fraud

Only accept Gulf job offers via licensed channels:

  • OEC (Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment Promoters)
  • POE-registered Overseas Employment Promoters — verify license on bemb.gov.pk
  • Direct employer offers from established GCC firms

Unregistered "agents" charging PKR 200,000-500,000 for promised Gulf jobs are a major source of fraud.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the full Gulf work visa process take?

+

Realistic end-to-end is 8-16 weeks: 2-3 weeks for employer demand letter, 4-6 weeks Pakistani attestation, 1-2 weeks GAMCA medical, 1-3 weeks embassy stamping, 1 week POE Protector + travel.

Can I work in Gulf without using a Pakistani agent?

+

Yes, if you have a direct job offer from a Gulf employer. Skilled professionals (engineers, doctors, IT, finance) often secure direct offers. Lower-skilled positions typically go through Pakistani Overseas Employment Promoters.

What is the maximum legal fee a Pakistani agent can charge?

+

Pakistani law caps OEP fees based on job category and destination, typically PKR 50,000-200,000. Agents charging significantly more are violating the law. Always demand a receipt and verify the OEP license on bemb.gov.pk.

Can I bring my family on a Gulf work visa?

+

Yes in most cases, IF your salary meets the minimum threshold (SAR 3,000+/month Saudi; AED 4,000+/month UAE; QAR 10,000+/month Qatar for white-collar). Lower-paid workers (construction, domestic) usually cannot.

Why is the POE Protector stamp mandatory?

+

Pakistan Emigration Ordinance requires the Protector stamp to ensure workers are protected by Pakistani labour rights frameworks, can access support if abused abroad, and have a legal record of their overseas employment.
