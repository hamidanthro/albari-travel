UK Standard Visitor Visa Checklist for Pakistani Applicants (2026)

Last updated: 12 July 2026

By Al Bari Travel & Tours · 24 June 2026 · 3 min read

The UK Standard Visitor Visa lets a Pakistani national visit the UK for tourism, family visit, business meetings, short-term study, or medical treatment for up to 6 months. Pakistani applications face moderate-to-high rejection rates — strong documentation and clear ties to Pakistan are essential. This guide covers everything needed.

What the Standard Visitor Visa permits

  • Tourism (sightseeing, shopping, attractions)
  • Visit family or friends
  • Business meetings, conferences, training
  • Short courses lasting under 6 months
  • Medical treatment (private)
  • Recreational activities (cricket matches, weddings, religious events)

What it does NOT permit

  • Working (paid or unpaid except specific business activities)
  • Studying at school or accredited courses over 6 months
  • Accessing public funds (NHS for medical issues unrelated to your visit)
  • Living in the UK through frequent or successive visits

Required documents

1. Passport

  • Original MRP/e-Passport valid for whole intended trip
  • At least one blank page

2. Confirmed return travel

  • Return flight booking confirmation
  • UK accommodation: hotel booking, Airbnb, or invitation letter from UK-based host

3. Financial evidence

  • Last 6 months of Pakistani bank statements (bank-stamped)
  • Salary slips (last 3 months) if employed
  • Tax returns / FBR NTN certificate
  • Property documents (if any)
  • Approximate guideline: enough funds to cover the trip + GBP 50-80 per day living costs + return home

4. Employment proof

  • NOC from employer on company letterhead stating leave dates, position, salary, and confirmed return to post
  • For business owners: company registration, recent business bank statement, FBR documents
  • For students: enrollment certificate + parent affidavit

5. Travel history

  • Photocopies of pages showing previous visa stamps (Saudi, UAE, Schengen, etc.)
  • Establishes you have a pattern of legal international travel and return

6. Family ties documentation

  • NADRA Computerised Marriage Certificate (if married)
  • Children's NADRA birth certificates (if you have children)
  • Establishes you have responsibilities pulling you home

7. UK sponsor invitation (if visiting family)

  • Letter from UK-based family / friend inviting you
  • Their UK immigration status (citizen/ILR/visa copy)
  • Their UK address proof (utility bill or council tax)
  • Their financial capacity to host you (if they're sponsoring your accommodation/expenses)
  • NADRA proof of relationship to UK sponsor (if family)

8. Polio + meningococcal vaccination cards

Polio mandatory for all Pakistanis entering UK from 2024. Meningococcal recommended.

9. TB test certificate

Required for stays over 6 months but recommended for all Pakistani applicants. IOM Karachi / Islamabad / Lahore.

Application process

  1. Apply online at gov.uk — select "Standard Visitor Visa"
  2. Pay visa fee GBP 127 for up to 6 months
  3. Book biometric appointment at VFS Karachi, Lahore, or Islamabad
  4. Attend biometric appointment with all original documents
  5. Wait 15 working days for standard processing (Priority and Super Priority available for extra fees)
  6. Collect passport with visa from VFS or have it couriered

Why Pakistani applications get refused

  1. Weak ties to Pakistan — the #1 reason. Single young applicants without family / employment / property face the most scrutiny.
  2. Inconsistent bank activity — large unexplained deposits just before application
  3. Unrealistic travel purpose — vague answers like "just visiting" without concrete plans
  4. UK sponsor whose own status is shaky — recent visa, unstable employment
  5. Previous overstay or visa refusal — major red flag
  6. Forged documents — instant 10-year ban

How to strengthen a Pakistani application

  • 6 months of consistent bank activity matching your stated income
  • Strong employment letter on company letterhead with HR contact
  • Property documents, business registration, family responsibilities all documented
  • Specific itinerary with named hotels and attractions
  • Refundable hotel bookings (Booking.com free cancellation)
  • Prior travel history to Saudi, UAE, Schengen, Turkey, Malaysia, Thailand — shows pattern of legal travel
  • Cover letter framing trip purpose and return intent

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does UK Visitor Visa take from Pakistan?

+

Standard processing: 15 working days. Priority service (GBP 500 extra): 5 working days. Super Priority (GBP 1,000 extra): 24 hours.

Can I stay in the UK longer than 6 months on a visit visa?

+

No. Standard Visitor Visa is strictly 6 months maximum per visit. Long-term multi-entry visas (2/5/10 years) allow multiple 6-month visits but each individual visit cannot exceed 6 months.

Can I get medical treatment in the UK on visit visa?

+

Yes for private medical treatment (you pay full cost). NHS treatment requires separate arrangements. Specify medical purpose at application and provide doctor referral, treatment plan, and proof of funds.

Does invitation from a UK family member guarantee approval?

+

No. UK invitations help but the consular officer assesses YOUR ties to Pakistan, your financial capacity, and your travel history. A UK invitation supports the case; it does not override weaknesses in your own profile.

Can I extend my visitor visa once I am in the UK?

+

No, generally not. Standard Visitor Visa cannot be extended in-country. You must leave the UK before the visa expires and apply again from Pakistan for a new visit.
