PDF / Web Form Ministry of Religious Affairs Pakistan

Pakistan Hajj 2027 Application Form (Government Scheme)

The official Pakistan Government Hajj Scheme application form for Hajj 1448H / 2027. Issued by the Ministry of Religious Affairs and Inter-faith Harmony (MoRA). Required for the November–December 2026 application window, with lottery (Qura) results announced in December 2026. The form requires CNIC details, passport details, banking information, vaccination records, and (for women under 45) Mahram details with nikah-nama or birth-certificate proof of relationship.

Mahram Affidavit Template (Hajj/Umrah for Women)

Affidavit template for Pakistani women under 45 travelling to Saudi Arabia for Hajj or Umrah with a Mahram. Documents the relationship (father, brother, husband, or son), travel dates, and joint accommodation. Required by the Pakistan Government Hajj Scheme and many private Hajj Group Operators. Should be printed on Rs. 50 stamp paper, signed by both parties, and notarised by an Oath Commissioner. Saudi authorities allow women aged 45+ to travel for Hajj without Mahram in supervised groups since 2021.

Polio Vaccination Yellow Card (Pakistan → Saudi Arabia)

Information sheet explaining the mandatory polio vaccination booster for all Pakistani travellers to Saudi Arabia. The booster is free at any government EPI (Expanded Programme on Immunization) centre in Pakistan and must be administered between 28 days and 12 months before arrival in Saudi. The yellow vaccination card is checked at Jeddah and Madinah immigration. Major EPI centres in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Peshawar, and Quetta are listed.

PDF / Web Form European Commission / VFS Global

Schengen Visa Application Form (for Pakistani Applicants)

Standard Schengen visa application form, harmonised across all 27 Schengen countries. Pakistani applicants apply through VFS Global (for France, Germany, Italy, Switzerland) or BLS International (for Spain) in Karachi, Lahore, or Islamabad. The form covers personal details, passport details, intended travel dates, accommodation, financial sponsor, and travel history. Must be filled in capital letters, signed, and submitted with all supporting documents.

Web Lookup Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment, Pakistan

POE Protector Stamp Application (Pakistan → Gulf Work)

Information on the mandatory Protector of Emigrants (POE) stamp for every Pakistani going abroad on a work visa. Issued by the Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment (BEMB) at one of 8 POE offices (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Multan, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Quetta, Faisalabad). Without the stamp, Pakistani immigration will block you from boarding the flight. Fee: PKR 1,000. Validity: 60 days from issuance.

Pakistani Document Forms

Pakistan passport renewal, NADRA CNIC corrections, computerised marriage and birth certificates, and police clearance certificate application forms.

Web Form Directorate General of Immigration & Passports, Pakistan

Pakistan Passport Renewal Form (MRP / e-Passport)

Information and application procedure for Pakistan Machine-Readable Passport (MRP) and e-Passport renewal. Apply online at dgip.gov.pk, book an appointment at any DGI&P Passport Office in Pakistan, pay the bank challan, attend biometric capture, and collect on the scheduled date. 2026 fees: MRP 5-year PKR 4,500 (normal) / PKR 9,000 (fee priority). e-Passport approximately double.

Web Form National Database and Registration Authority

NADRA Computerised Marriage Certificate Application

Application procedure for the NADRA computerised marriage certificate, required for Saudi family visit/residence visas, UK spouse visas, US K-1 visas, and most international family applications. Apply at any NADRA Mega Centre with original Nikah-nama, both spouses' CNIC, and two witnesses' CNIC. Processing: 7–14 working days. Fee: PKR 250–500. The original Nikah-nama from your Nikah Registrar is not directly accepted by most foreign embassies — use the NADRA version.
