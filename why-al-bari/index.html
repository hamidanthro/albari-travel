Why choose Al Bari Travel &amp; Tours

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Honest comparison of Al Bari vs the Pakistan Government Hajj Scheme vs typical private Pakistani operators — pricing, services, group size, and what you actually get.

A 60-second summary

Pakistani travellers booking Umrah, Hajj, or international visas have three real options: the Pakistan Government Hajj Scheme (cheapest, lottery, basic), a typical private operator (more expensive, varies enormously in quality), or an agency like Al Bari that operates remote-first with named human representatives. None is right for everyone — this page lays out the honest trade-offs so you can pick the one that fits.

When the Government Scheme is the right choice

If your absolute top priority is cost and you're willing to accept lottery-based booking + larger group batches + less choice over hotel and airline, the Government Hajj Scheme is excellent value. We tell prospective clients this honestly — many of our team's own family members have performed Hajj through the Government Scheme.

When a typical private operator is the right choice

For families with strong local ties to a specific bazaar operator they trust through word-of-mouth, sticking with that operator can be fine. The risk is that quality varies enormously between private operators — some are excellent, some advertise 4-star hotels and deliver 2-star ones. Always demand the exact hotel name and metres from the Haram in writing before paying.

What we don't offer

In the spirit of honest comparison — here's what we deliberately do NOT do:

  • Walk-in offices. Remote-first is our model. If you specifically need bazaar in-person service, we're not the right fit.
  • Race-to-the-bottom pricing. We don't undercut by skimping on hotel proximity or transport quality. If a quote elsewhere is 30% cheaper, ask exactly what's being cut.
  • Mass-group Hajj (100+ pilgrims per leader). Our private Hajj groups stay under 40.
  • Booking before confirmed quota. We don't sell Hajj seats we haven't confirmed yet.
  • Hotel reservations as a stand-alone service. We bundle hotels into Umrah/Hajj packages but don't separately book hotel-only stays.

Common questions about choosing an operator

Is Al Bari Travel licensed by MoRA?

+

Yes, Al Bari Travel & Tours operates as a licensed Pakistani travel agency. For Hajj packages we work through the MoRA-licensed Hajj Group Operator system. Always ask any operator to show their MoRA HGO license number before paying a Hajj deposit — we welcome this verification.

How do you keep prices fair without a walk-in office?

+

Walk-in offices add PKR 200,000+/month in rent + staff to an operator's overhead, which is passed to customers. We save that cost by operating remote-first via phone, WhatsApp, and email. The savings go into hotel quality and smaller group sizes, not into our pocket.

Can I see real customer reviews?

+

We're actively building our Trustpilot presence and Google Business Profile reviews. We deliberately do not display fabricated testimonials — only verified reviews from real customers will be surfaced here. In the meantime, ask us for referral phone numbers of past clients in your city; we connect prospects directly with returning pilgrims wherever possible.

What if I'm not happy with my package?

+

All issues during the trip are escalated to your Regional Representative immediately by WhatsApp. We've handled wrong-hotel placements, delayed visas, lost-passport emergencies, and medical situations — usually within hours. Pakistani Embassy in Riyadh and Consulate in Jeddah are our escalation paths for emergencies.

How do I switch from the Government Scheme to a private package if I'm not selected?

+

Yes — many Pakistani applicants apply to the Government Scheme first and switch to a private operator (us or anyone else) if not selected in the December lottery. Government Scheme deposits are refundable to non-selected applicants. Don't wait until February if you're concerned about availability; private quotas often fill by then.
