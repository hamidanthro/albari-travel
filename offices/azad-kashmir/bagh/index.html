Booking from Bagh — FAQ

Can I get international flights booked from Bagh?

+

Yes. Al Bari Travel & Tours books international flights from any major Pakistani airport for clients across Bagh and the wider Azad Jammu & Kashmir region — including Saudi Arabia, UAE, and other destinations. Ticket delivery is digital.

What is the best time of year to book Umrah from Bagh?

+

Umrah from Bagh runs year-round. Off-peak months (mid-September through October, late February through April) offer the best rates and least crowded conditions in Makkah. Ramadan and the weeks around Hajj are most expensive. Haiwad Ahmad can advise on the optimal window for your situation.

What payment options does Al Bari Travel accept from Bagh clients?

+

We accept cash, bank transfer, JazzCash, and Easypaisa from clients in Bagh. Bookings are confirmed once a deposit is received; the balance is due before the visa submission. Haiwad Ahmad will walk you through the schedule on the first call.

What documents are needed for a Saudi visa from Bagh?

+

For Umrah and Hajj from Bagh we typically need: a valid passport (6+ months), recent photos, NIC copy, and travel proof. Our Saudi visa processing handles the rest — application, fees, and tracking. Call +92 315 9596161 for the current checklist.
